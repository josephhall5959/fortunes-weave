FORTUNE'S WEAVE · INSIDE THE ENGINE

Everything you need to check it

The engine has never seen the game run. These are the rules and numbers it uses, laid out so anyone with a save file can spot where it goes wrong.

1 · Combat formulas

Attack speed (AS) = Spd − max(0, weapon Wt − Build) Build = 4 (+5 / +10 with Build skills) Follow-up if your AS − foe AS ≥ 4 (integrated over the spread of your Spd, so near-threshold units double sometimes) Atk = Str (or Mag) + weapon Mt × effectiveness (×2, or ×3 for "Eff.+") + skill/art bonuses Damage per hit = Atk − foe Def (or Res for magic), never below 0; ×0.5 vs undead unless the weapon negates Underworld Boon Hit % = weapon Hit + Dex + skill/weapon-rank bonuses − foe Avoid Avoid = foe AS (+ terrain, bosses only) Crit % = (Dex + Lck) / 2 + weapon Crit + bonuses − foe Lck / 2 a crit does ×3 damage Enemy crit = the same formula − 30 (reported flat penalty on enemy crits) Weapon-rank bonus = D+ Hit+3 · C+ Hit+5 · B+ Hit/Avo+5 · A+ Hit/Avo+7 · S Hit/Avo+10 (highest level only)

Sources: Game8 and Fextralife stat pages and community spreadsheets. Hit and crit coefficients are not officially published; one in-game forecast with known stats would confirm or fix them. Combat arts, personal skills, learned passives, battalions, mounts and Divine class abilities modify these numbers as their in-game text says.

2 · Enemies

Late-game enemies scale with your army. Each enemy type is modeled as an average character (the community sheet's average growths and bases) who walked a standard path into a master class, three levels above your units (Hard). Half of all regular enemies are treated as undead (they take half damage unless the weapon negates it).

3 · Characters: growths, path and projected stats

Pick a character. The engine projects stats along the default class path (the start class from the planner, then the most natural next class at each tier). The ± number is the spread from level-up luck: about two in three playthroughs land within it. If your unit is far outside on a similar path, the growths or path are off.

5 · Scenarios

Map

8 player-phase attacks per map, enemies drawn evenly from the 16 types; then an enemy phase where two foes of one type attack (HP carries over). Durability and spell uses are a budget for the whole map.

Boss turn

One player phase against a private set of late-game bosses, weighted by difficulty. A boss has health gems and one character breaks at most one per turn. Bosses hold favorable terrain (Avoid +20).

Support

8 actions per map spent greedily on heals, Dance, Warp/Rescue, buffs and foe control, with caps on how much of each a map can use. Value = what it adds to a reference team.

Dungeon clash

No grid; one shared party HP pool; no recovery between clashes. Party of 5 rotating actions vs. 3 foes (203 HP). Recovery Links heal (3 per dungeon). Details.

Two builds: as handed over (default path, Iron-tier gear, no arts; used for map play) and planned (a chosen path from level 1, refined gear, all arts and skills; used for bosses, support and clashes).

6 · Known unknowns

  • Hit, avoid and crit coefficients, the enemy crit penalty, and Build growth are community-reported or assumed.
  • Enemy stat lines are modeled (an average character on a standard path), not read from the game.
  • Clash formulas aren't published: enemy group size, overkill, Link Points and Recovery Link amounts are assumptions.
  • One source conflict is unresolved: The Eternal's Cha growth (10 or 0).
  • Personal skills are assumed upgraded in planned builds.
